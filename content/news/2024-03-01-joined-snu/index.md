In March 2024, Prof. Jong-Seong Kug joined the **School of Earth and Environmental Sciences at Seoul National
University**, after serving as a professor at the Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH) since 2014.
The Climate System Laboratory continues its research on climate change, El Niño, and the carbon cycle at SNU.
