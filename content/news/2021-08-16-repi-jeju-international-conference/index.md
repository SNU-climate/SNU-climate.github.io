The international conference 'Regional Challenges in the 21st Century' was held over two days, on the 19th and 20th, at Ocean Suites Jeju. It was hosted by the Regional Economy and Policy Institute (REPI) of Daegu University together with the Korea Regional Development Association and the SNU BK21 Plus regional quantitative analysis group, and organized by the Korea Research Institute for Human Settlements and the POSTECH Division of Environmental Science and Engineering.
