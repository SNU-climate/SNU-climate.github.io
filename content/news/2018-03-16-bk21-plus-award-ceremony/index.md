2018.03.16 2017 BK21 Plus Outstanding Researcher Award ceremony

Ph.D. student Jinsoo Kim, a participant in the BK21 Plus 'Environment–Energy–Climate System' research team, was selected as an outstanding BK21 Plus researcher for 2017 and received a commendation from the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Education. He was one of 11 awardees in engineering out of 33 in total, and was chosen to present his research at the ceremony as a representative achievement. Key achievements: four papers in top climate journals, including two first-author papers in Nature Geoscience and Nature Communications.
