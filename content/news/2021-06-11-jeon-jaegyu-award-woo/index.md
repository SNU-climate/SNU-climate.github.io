Dr. Sung-Ho Woo of Prof. Kug's lab (POSTECH Division of Environmental Science and Engineering) received the Excellence Award. The Korea Polar Research Institute presents the award every year in honor of the late Jeon Jae-Gyu, a member of the 17th wintering team at King Sejong Station who died while searching for a missing colleague. Dr. Woo was recognized for his research on polar–midlatitude interactions and on East Asian climate prediction related to cryospheric variability.
