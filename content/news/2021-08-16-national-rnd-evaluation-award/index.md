At the 2018 National R&D Performance Evaluation awards hosted by the Ministry of Science and ICT, Prof. Kug received the Presidential Citation. He was recognized for his research on El Niño, the relationship between phytoplankton and Arctic warming, and other major aspects of climate change and variability. The ceremony was held on the 28th at the Government Complex Gwacheon.
